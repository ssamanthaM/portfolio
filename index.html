01Handa

Handa

AI legal aid for overseas Filipino workers.

A mobile app that walks a migrant worker through a guided intake in their own language, maps their situation to the right legal remedy in their home country, stores contracts and pay records in a secure vault, and routes the case to a partner pro-bono organization.

Built solo end to end: product definition, partner ecosystem, information architecture, and code. The design constraint that shaped everything — an app used by people under employer surveillance cannot leak. Data minimization and on-device storage came before features.

02Agent
Live in production

Autonomous storefront agent

A design goes in one end; a published, screened listing comes out the other.

An unattended pipeline running a live print-on-demand shop. A folder watcher picks up a new design, Claude Vision reads it and picks the products it suits, the agent generates SEO-optimized copy, waits on mockup rendering, and publishes to the storefront. Every run is logged to SQLite; results arrive by Telegram. A companion dashboard scores demand signals week over week and feeds the results back into what the agent is permitted to make.

The part I’d point a governance team to is the pre-publish gate: nothing ships without clearing trademark screening, duplicate detection, and a demand check against live market data, then routing to PASS / REVIEW / BLOCK. It is a working instance of the thing policy documents usually only describe — a human-reviewable checkpoint between an autonomous model and an irreversible action.

03WPS
Private build

WPS Incident Record

Coercion at sea, coded against the law it breaks.

A structured evidentiary database that codes Chinese coercive activity in the West Philippine Sea against the specific UNCLOS provisions and 2016 arbitral award findings it implicates — turning a decade of dispersed incident reporting into a record built to withstand legal scrutiny.

It ingests Philippine Coast Guard and AFP incident reports, National Maritime Council statements, diplomatic protests, released imagery, and live AIS from aisstream.io and Global Fishing Watch, then produces structured case records. Extraction is model-assisted and every record passes human adjudication before entering the database, with sourcing and chain of custody documented to the Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations.

Role
Sole builder
Stack
Cloudflare Workers · aisstream.io live AIS · Global Fishing Watch API · Claude API for structured extraction with human adjudication · authenticated access layer

04Fluency
Live

AI Fluency OS

The curriculum I built to close my own technical gap.

A deployed web app that structures sixty days of AI study across four parallel tracks — concepts, technical, theory, policy — and enforces one fixed daily ritual: twenty minutes learning a concept, twenty building something, twenty reading a primary source. Progress persists server-side and syncs across devices.

I built it because the available options were either too shallow to survive a technical meeting or too deep to finish. The policy track — EU AI Act, NIST, frontier safety frameworks — is rebuilt on technical footing rather than treated as separate literacy.

05Ma Biche

Ma Biche

French immersion chosen by mood, not by level.

A daily French listening and reading companion — podcasts, music, radio, literature — curated by mood rather than sorted into difficulty tiers. A new selection every day, so deciding what to listen to never becomes the reason not to.

Built to keep a third language alive without a course structure. One editorial judgment, executed cleanly: maintaining fluency is a habit problem, not a content problem.
